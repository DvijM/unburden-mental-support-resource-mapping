Platform Governance

Terms of Use & Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

1. Educational & Self-Screening Purpose Only

Unburden is an independent educational mental health support and resource-mapping platform aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 3, Target 3.4. None of the tools, questionnaires (including PHQ-9, GAD-7, and WHO-5), soundtracks, or articles on this website constitute a medical diagnosis, psychotherapy, or clinical treatment advice.

2. Not an Emergency Service

Unburden does not perform live crisis monitoring or emergency dispatch. Selecting a high score or indicating thoughts of self-harm on a self-check triggers immediate on-screen links to crisis helplines, but no clinician is automatically alerted. In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

3. Age Eligibility

Account registration and standardized self-assessments on this platform are intended for individuals aged 13 and older.

4. Role Applications & Responder Conduct

Applicants for Responder, Analyst, or Content Editor roles must provide truthful institutional and professional details. Approved Responders may access individual user records solely within the scope of an active user-granted Share Permission and must treat all shared information with strict confidentiality.

5. Instrument Attribution

The Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9) was developed by Drs. Robert L. Spitzer, Janet B.W. Williams, Kurt Kroenke, and colleagues with an educational grant from Pfizer Inc. (Kroenke, Spitzer, & Williams, Journal of General Internal Medicine, 2001). No permission is required to reproduce, translate, display, or distribute the PHQ-9.
