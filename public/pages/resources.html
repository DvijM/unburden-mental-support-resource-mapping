Sourcing Approach

Written With Reference to Public Health Guidelines

Content in the Unburden catalog is written and curated with reference to publicly available World Health Organization (WHO) and American Psychological Association (APA) guidance, and aligned with the United Nations SDG 3 framework. It is intended for educational awareness, not as a substitute for individualized clinical review.

  • ✓ Aligned with UN SDG 3 framework
  • ✓ Reviewed periodically for accuracy

A Note on This Content

These resources are educational, not diagnostic. If you're concerned about your mental health, a licensed clinician can give you guidance this catalog can't.
