Sourcing Approach

Written With Reference to Public Health Guidelines

Content in the Unburden catalog is written and curated with reference to publicly available World Health Organization (WHO) and American Psychological Association (APA) guidance, and aligned with the United Nations SDG 3.4 framework. It is intended for educational awareness, not as a substitute for individualized clinical review.
