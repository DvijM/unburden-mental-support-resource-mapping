Support Field Guide

Demystifying Crisis Support: What to Expect

Reaching out during distress takes courage. Knowing what happens when you call or text a crisis helpline can make taking the first step easier.

1. Direct & Confidential

Unburden never asks for your personal details before connecting you to a helpline. You can share only what you feel comfortable sharing with the helpline responder.

2. Active, Non-Judgmental Listening

Trained responders listen without judgment, help you slow down overwhelming thoughts, and focus on your immediate safety and comfort.

3. Collaborative Next Steps

Together, you can identify practical coping steps for the next few hours and explore local community or clinical support options.
