Privacy-by-Default Architecture

  • Unbundled Granular Consent

    Account creation never forces you to store assessment scores or share data. Every optional category has its own toggle.

  • Strict Role Separation

    Super Administrators only see aggregate statistics. Individual records can only be viewed by a Responder you explicitly authorize.

  • Export & One-Click Erasure

    Download all your data as JSON or permanently erase your account and records at any time from your Privacy Dashboard.
