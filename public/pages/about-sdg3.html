The Goal

What is SDG 3?

In 2015, all United Nations Member States adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, with 17 goals to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure prosperity for all. Goal 3, “Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages,” covers maternal and child health, infectious disease, non-communicable disease, substance abuse, and universal health coverage.

Mental health sits within this goal because it shapes how people learn, work, and care for each other. Depression and anxiety alone are estimated by the World Health Organization to cost the global economy around US$1 trillion each year in lost productivity, and suicide remains a leading cause of death among young people.

The Target

Target 3.4 in plain language

“By 2030, reduce by one third premature mortality from non-communicable diseases through prevention and treatment and promote mental health and well-being.”

In practice, that means earlier recognition of distress, reachable care, less stigma, and support that works for people regardless of where they live or what they can afford. Those are the gaps Unburden tries to narrow.

Why We Built This

Our mission: make the first step toward support easier

Many people who struggle never reach care. They don’t know where to start, can’t tell whether what they feel is serious, or face cost, distance, or stigma. Unburden is a free, approachable starting point that connects people to trustworthy information and the right help, in line with Target 3.4.
