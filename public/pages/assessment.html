Educational Screening Disclaimer & Age Notice

This self-check uses the standardized Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9; Kroenke, Spitzer, & Williams, 2001) and is validated for adults and adolescents aged 13 and older. It is a self-screening tool, not a medical or psychiatric diagnosis, and does not replace an evaluation by a qualified doctor or mental health professional.
