Role-Based Access Governance

Administrative & Responder Role Application

To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive records, public accounts cannot self-assign privileged roles. Verified support professionals, researchers, and directory editors may apply below. Approved applications receive a single-use cryptographic invitation token.

1. Submit Role Application

Lifecycle: Submitted → Pending Review → Approved / Rejected by a Super Administrator.

2. Check Application Status

Enter your application email (and optional Application ID) to inspect review progress.

3. Activate Approved Invitation

Have a single-use invitation token from a Super Administrator? Verify your email and set your account password below.
